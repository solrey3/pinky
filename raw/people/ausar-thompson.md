# Ausar Thompson

**Born:** January 30, 2003
**Nationality:** American
**Position:** G/F
**Active:** 2023–present
**NBA team at draft:** Detroit Pistons

## Summary

Ausar Thompson is a Detroit Pistons player drafted fifth overall in 2023, immediately after his twin brother Amen. The brothers came through Overtime Elite instead of college basketball and became the first brothers selected in the top five of the same NBA draft. Ausar is noted for defense, rebounding, and athletic wing play.

## Career Highlights

- Won Overtime Elite titles with Team Elite in 2022 and the City Reapers alongside Amen in 2023; earned Finals MVP honors both times and league MVP in 2023.
- Selected fifth overall by Detroit in the 2023 NBA draft, one pick after Amen.
- His 2023–24 NBA rookie season ended early after treatment for a blood clot.

## Why They Matter

Ausar's Overtime Elite success and top-five NBA selection highlight the same non-college development path as his brother's. His defensive and rebounding ability helped him contribute as an NBA rookie.
