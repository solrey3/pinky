# Amen Thompson

**Born:** January 30, 2003
**Nationality:** American
**Position:** G/F
**Active:** 2023–present
**NBA team at draft:** Houston Rockets

## Summary

Amen Thompson is a Houston Rockets player drafted fourth overall in 2023. He and his twin brother Ausar came through Overtime Elite instead of college basketball and became the first brothers selected in the top five of the same NBA draft. Amen's athleticism, defense, rebounding, and playmaking define his game.

## Career Highlights

- Won the 2022–23 Overtime Elite title with the City Reapers alongside Ausar; named to the All-OTE First Team.
- Selected fourth overall by Houston in the 2023 NBA draft, one pick before Ausar.
- Named to the NBA All-Rookie Second Team in 2024 and All-Defensive First Team in 2025.

## Why They Matter

The twins' consecutive top-five selections made Overtime Elite a visible alternative development path to college basketball. Amen's early NBA honors underscore his defensive impact and broader potential as a versatile wing/guard.
