My mother, 91, has dementia. Every bank says I need her signature to unlock her $100,000 stock certificate. What can I do?

‘These shares are the only assets in her estate without designated beneficiaries’

Aug. 27, 2026, 5:15 a.m. ET

Dear Quentin,

My 91-year-old mother is bedridden in an assisted-living memory-care facility in Virginia.

She owns about $100,000 of Consolidated Edison stock that is held at a company I’ll call Acme Corp., a well-known stock-transfer agent, as a paper stock certificate. I hold durable power of attorney for her and am the named executor of her will.
