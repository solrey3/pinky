# Critical Rationalism

Popper's account of rational inquiry as the public criticism of conjectures. Knowledge grows not by securing final foundations but by proposing bold ideas, testing them severely, eliminating error, and keeping even successful theories provisional.

## Notes

Critical rationalism is a useful operating norm for a knowledge base: preserve claims, expose them to challenge, update cross-links, and treat contradictions as opportunities to improve the graph rather than as failures to hide.
