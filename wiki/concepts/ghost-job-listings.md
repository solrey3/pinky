# Ghost Job Listings

Ghost job listings are advertisements for positions that an employer may not currently intend to fill. They can distort labor-demand signals, waste applicants' time, collect résumés for future use, or create a misleading appearance of organizational growth.

## Notes

The policy problem is partly one of provenance: job boards can verify that an employer posted an ad, but not necessarily that a funded, active vacancy exists. Useful rules would therefore distinguish evergreen recruiting from knowingly deceptive listings and require clear status or expiration signals.
