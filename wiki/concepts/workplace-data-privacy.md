# Workplace Data Privacy

Workplace data privacy governs how employers and third parties collect, transfer, combine, and reuse employee records, especially where workers have limited bargaining power or meaningful ability to consent.

## Notes

A lawful data transfer may still create governance risk when the receiving organization can infer new facts, combine records with other datasets, or use them beyond the purpose for which workers supplied them.
