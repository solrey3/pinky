# Cancer Prevention

Cancer prevention reduces incidence through modifiable-risk interventions such as tobacco control, vaccination, infection treatment, occupational and environmental protections, healthier behavior, and clinically appropriate screening or preventive care. Population-level preventability estimates depend strongly on geography, cancer type, time horizon, exposure definitions, and assumptions about intervention uptake.

## Notes

Preventable does not mean individually avoidable: population-attributable risk estimates describe modeled reductions under changed exposures and policy, not blame or certainty for a particular diagnosis.
