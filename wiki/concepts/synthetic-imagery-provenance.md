# Synthetic Imagery Provenance

Synthetic imagery provenance is the practice of labeling, tracing, and constraining AI-generated or AI-altered images so users can distinguish observed imagery from generated artifacts.

## Notes

The common risk is not merely collection or generation; it is downstream trust. Once a plate read, enforcement record, map image, or photograph is treated as evidence, provenance failures become institutional failures.

Reference-based verification can complement watermarking: watermarking marks generated or edited media, while a trusted reference can support a positive authenticity claim. Both still depend on interoperable standards, secure capture and signing, durable metadata, and tamper-resistant verification.
