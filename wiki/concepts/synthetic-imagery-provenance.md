# Synthetic Imagery Provenance

Synthetic imagery provenance is the practice of labeling, tracing, and constraining AI-generated or AI-altered images so users can distinguish observed imagery from generated artifacts.

## Sources

- [2026-07-31: Evening Brief — Friday, July 31, 2026](../sources/newsletter-2026-07-31-evening.md) — Google Earth’s retracted fake-satellite-imagery tool shows that maps and remote-sensing outputs now need visible AI-generation provenance.

## Notes

The common risk is not merely collection or generation; it is downstream trust. Once a plate read, enforcement record, or map image is treated as evidence, provenance failures become institutional failures.
