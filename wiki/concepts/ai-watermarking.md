# AI Watermarking

AI watermarking is the practice of embedding detectable signals in generated media or model outputs to support provenance, labeling, and abuse investigation.

## Notes

Invisible text markers may support attribution and abuse investigation, but detection durability, false positives, interoperability, disclosure, and survival through editing or translation determine whether they provide evidence rather than merely signaling policy intent. Removal scams add a second-order risk: users may be defrauded by tools claiming to defeat a marker regardless of whether those tools work.

This concept is useful when AI, documents, identity, or provenance controls turn ordinary content surfaces into security or trust boundaries.
