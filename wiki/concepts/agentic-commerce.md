# Agentic Commerce

Agentic commerce is the use of software agents to search, compare, negotiate, select, or purchase goods and services on a person's or organization's behalf. It turns delegated intent into a payment and identity problem: an agent must prove who authorized it, what scope and budget it has, and whether a transaction reflects current user intent.

## Notes

Useful controls include narrowly scoped credentials, transaction and merchant limits, explicit confirmation thresholds, revocation, tamper-evident receipts, and clear liability and dispute rules. Conventional fraud detection remains necessary, but it does not by itself prove that an autonomous purchase matched the principal's intent.
