# Data Residency

Data residency is the requirement or user choice that digital information be stored and processed within a specified geographic or legal jurisdiction.

## Notes

Regional hosting can support regulatory, procurement, latency, and sovereignty requirements, but location alone does not establish privacy; access controls, encryption, subprocessors, and applicable law remain relevant.
