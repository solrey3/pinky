# Human-in-the-Loop AI Education

An educational model in which AI supplies explanations, practice, or individualized tutoring while teachers retain responsibility for goals, context, verification, and intervention.

## Sources

- [2026-08-08: Noon Dispatch — Saturday, August 8, 2026](../sources/newsletter-2026-08-08-noon.md) — Sal Khan argues that classroom AI works best with an adult at the wheel.

## Notes

The source treats supervision as part of the instructional design, not merely a fallback after model failure. AI can scale practice and feedback, but educators remain accountable for judgment and student welfare.
