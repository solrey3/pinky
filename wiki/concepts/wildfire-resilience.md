# Wildfire Resilience

Wildfire resilience is the ability of communities, land-management systems, and infrastructure networks to reduce wildfire risk, operate through fire disruptions, and recover when evacuation, smoke, heat, or physical damage interrupt normal service.

## Notes

The useful frame is operational: wildfire is not only a disaster headline. It shapes grazing and vegetation policy, road and evacuation logistics, telecom and space-communications continuity, insurance exposure, public-health smoke risk, and regional energy demand.
