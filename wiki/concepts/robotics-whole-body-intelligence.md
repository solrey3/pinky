# Robotics Whole-Body Intelligence

Robotics whole-body intelligence is the idea that useful robots need integrated perception, planning, balance, locomotion, manipulation, and task reasoning rather than a language model bolted onto a single actuator or scripted workflow.

## Notes

The safety and deployment issue is capability integration. Once perception, planning, and motion are joined, failures can move from bad text to physical-world consequences, making evaluation, simulation, fallback behavior, and human override central.
