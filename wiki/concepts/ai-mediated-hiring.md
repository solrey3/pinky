# AI-Mediated Hiring

The use of AI by employers, recruiters, and applicants to create, screen, rank, or evaluate applications and interviews. It can reduce administrative work, but also creates authenticity, bias, transparency, and accountability problems when automated signals shape access to employment.

## Notes

AI-mediated hiring is broader than algorithmic bias: it includes applicant-side generation, employer-side screening, authenticity checks, and the feedback loop created when both parties optimize against automated systems.
