# Hypothetico-Deductive Method

A model of scientific reasoning in which researchers propose hypotheses, deduce observable consequences, and test those consequences against experience. In Popper's version, the important tests are risky ones that could refute the theory.

## Notes

The method is portable beyond laboratory science: make a claim explicit, derive what should happen if it is true, define failure conditions, then let reality push back.
