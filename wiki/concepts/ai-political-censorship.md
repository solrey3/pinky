# AI Political Censorship

AI political censorship is the deliberate restriction, reframing, or refusal of model outputs to enforce an authority's permitted political narratives. It differs from ordinary safety moderation when controls primarily protect a government, party, or ideology from scrutiny rather than users from harm.
