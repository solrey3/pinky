# Groundwater Contamination

Groundwater contamination is the presence of hazardous chemicals, industrial residues, fuel compounds, or other pollutants in subsurface water. The governance problem is evidence-heavy: testing methods, sampling locations, thresholds, exposure pathways, and community health reports all matter.
