# Tokenized Securities

Tokenized securities are regulated financial instruments whose ownership or transfer is represented on programmable digital ledgers. Tokenization can support faster settlement, fractional ownership, automated corporate actions, and longer trading windows, but it does not eliminate securities law, custody obligations, market surveillance, or issuer and intermediary responsibilities.

## Notes

The critical design questions are legal and operational as much as technical: who controls the authoritative ownership record, how custody and identity work, when settlement becomes final, how errors or theft are handled, how corporate actions propagate, and whether fragmented venues preserve fair pricing and investor protection. Twenty-four-hour availability also changes liquidity, staffing, surveillance, and operational-resilience requirements rather than simply extending today's session.
