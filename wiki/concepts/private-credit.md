# Private Credit

Privately negotiated lending conducted outside public bond markets and conventional bank loans, commonly through asset managers and specialized funds. It can broaden access to capital and tailor loan terms, but limited price discovery, illiquidity, leverage, valuation discretion, and weaker public disclosure can obscure correlated credit risk.
