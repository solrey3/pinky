# AI Battlefield Autonomy

AI battlefield autonomy is the use of machine-learning systems to perceive, track, select, navigate toward, or otherwise support engagement decisions in military environments. In this wiki, the concept matters when autonomy moves from lab capability into deployed weapons logistics, target tracking, command accountability, or escalation risk.

## Notes

The key distinction is not whether a weapon contains software; it is whether perception, tracking, intelligence assessment, or engagement-adjacent judgment shifts from a human-operated tool to an autonomous or semi-autonomous system whose errors, escalation pathways, and accountability chain must be governed. Human authorization alone is inadequate when machine-generated evidence lacks provenance or independent corroboration.
