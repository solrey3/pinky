# Falsifiability

The property of a claim or theory that it could conflict with possible observations. For Popper, falsifiability is the key mark of empirical science: a theory must risk being wrong by ruling out some possible states of affairs.

## Notes

Falsifiability is not the same as being false. A strong scientific theory may be highly corroborated precisely because it has survived severe attempts to falsify it. The practical habit is to ask, before accepting a claim, what observable result would count against it.
