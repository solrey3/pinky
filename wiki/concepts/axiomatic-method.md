# Axiomatic Method

A way of organizing knowledge by stating primitive terms and assumptions, then deriving propositions through explicit rules of reasoning. Euclid's *Elements* is the classical model: definitions and postulates become the base from which theorems are built.

## Notes

The axiomatic method makes hidden assumptions visible. Its practical value extends beyond mathematics: define terms, name starting premises, preserve dependency trails, and make conclusions auditable.
