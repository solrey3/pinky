# AI Research Opacity

AI research opacity is the shift from open publication toward private, selectively disclosed frontier-lab work. The practical issue is not only academic access; it is whether outside researchers, policymakers, users, and competitors can inspect claims about capability, safety, benchmarks, and incident response.

## Notes

Research opacity compounds other trust problems: if frontier labs disclose less, then benchmark claims, safety boundaries, vulnerability-discovery claims, and incident timelines must carry more provenance burden.
