# AI-Powered Fraud Detection

AI-powered fraud detection uses machine-learning models, behavioral signals, device signals, and transaction patterns to identify suspicious activity faster than manual review. In the current dispatch context, it is framed as a defensive response to AI-enabled scams and social-engineering pressure on payments systems.
