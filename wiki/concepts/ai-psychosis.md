# AI Psychosis

AI psychosis is a shorthand for reports of delusion, dependency, or reality-distortion episodes associated with intensive chatbot interaction. It is not a formal diagnosis in this wiki; it is tracked as a human-factors and safety-boundary concern around AI systems.

## Sources

- [2026-07-26: Evening Brief — Sunday, July 26, 2026](../sources/newsletter-2026-07-26-evening.md) — NewsNation reports a man saying AI psychosis cost him his job, home, and sanity, moving AI safety from benchmark refusal behavior to downstream user harm.

## Notes

Use this concept cautiously: the term can sensationalize mental-health issues. The durable wiki question is what design, disclosure, escalation, and support duties AI systems have when users appear dependent, delusional, or at risk.
