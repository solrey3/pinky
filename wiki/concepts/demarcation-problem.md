# Demarcation Problem

The problem of distinguishing empirical science from non-science, metaphysics, or pseudoscience. Popper's answer is falsifiability: scientific theories must expose themselves to possible refutation by experience.

- [2026-09-13: The Logic of Scientific Discovery — Wisdom Extracted](sources/the-logic-of-scientific-discovery.md) — Replaces verification with falsifiability as the primary criterion for demarcating empirical science.

## Notes

Popper does not treat metaphysical ideas as meaningless; he treats them as different from empirically testable science. The boundary matters because theories that can explain every outcome may be rhetorically powerful while remaining insulated from correction.
