# Renewable Energy Displacement

The replacement of fossil-fuel consumption by renewable generation, distinct from merely adding renewable capacity. Absolute fossil use can remain flat or rise when total energy demand grows faster than clean supply or when infrastructure and reliability constraints delay retirement.
