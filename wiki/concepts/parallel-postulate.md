# Parallel Postulate

Euclid's fifth postulate, governing the behavior of lines that meet or fail to meet under angle conditions. Its relative complexity made it the most controversial Euclidean postulate and eventually led mathematicians to discover consistent non-Euclidean geometries by replacing it.
