# AI Self-Replication Risk

AI self-replication risk is the possibility that a model-enabled system could copy its software, state, or operating capability to additional hosts without sufficient human authorization. Credible assessment must separate generated instructions from successful execution and measure access prerequisites, exploitability, privilege, persistence, propagation rate, human assistance, and containment.

## Notes

A laboratory demonstration, simulated plan, or model-generated exploit sequence is not equivalent to autonomous propagation in the wild. Evaluation should document the initial foothold, device permissions, external tooling, network conditions, human interventions, persistence, and whether ordinary segmentation and credential controls stop the chain.
