# AI-Assisted Regulatory Decision-Making

AI-assisted regulatory decision-making is the use of machine-learning or generative systems to support government assessments, prioritization, enforcement, or rulemaking. Such tools may increase speed or consistency, but accountable use requires disclosed purposes, reliable evidence, human responsibility, audit trails, and avenues to challenge consequential outputs.
