# Ausar Thompson

American NBA player drafted fifth overall by the Detroit Pistons in 2023, one pick after his twin brother [[amen-thompson|Amen]]. Both came to the NBA through Overtime Elite rather than college basketball.

- **Born:** January 30, 2003
- **Team at draft:** Detroit Pistons
- **Draft:** 2023, No. 5 overall

## Career

- Won two Overtime Elite championships, with Team Elite in 2022 and the City Reapers alongside Amen in 2023; earned Finals MVP honors in both seasons and league MVP in 2023.
- Entered the NBA as an athletic wing noted for rebounding, defense, and transition play.
- His 2023–24 rookie season ended early after treatment for a blood clot.
