# Amen Thompson

American NBA player drafted fourth overall by the Houston Rockets in 2023, immediately before his twin brother [[ausar-thompson|Ausar]]. Both came to the NBA through Overtime Elite rather than college basketball.

- **Born:** January 30, 2003
- **Team at draft:** Houston Rockets
- **Draft:** 2023, No. 4 overall

## Career

- Played for the City Reapers with Ausar during Overtime Elite's 2022–23 championship season; earned All-OTE First Team honors.
- Made the NBA All-Rookie Second Team in 2024 and the NBA All-Defensive First Team in 2025.
- Known for athleticism, defensive versatility, rebounding, and playmaking from the perimeter.
