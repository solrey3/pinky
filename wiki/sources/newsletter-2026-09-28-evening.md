## Synthesis Notes

The FAA item joins earlier coverage of operational aviation AI but shifts the emphasis from deploying automation to certifying software embedded in aircraft. Both cases require bounded authority, traceable changes, independent testing, monitoring, and safe fallback behavior.

The self-improving-systems warning extends the wiki's model-safety thread from output guardrails and tool permissions toward recursive capability growth, oversight latency, measurable intervention thresholds, and enforceable deployment controls. Kalshi's reported loss reinforces that prediction markets cannot be interpreted apart from jurisdiction and gambling-law classification.

The market close reverses the same day's earlier concentrated technology strength: all four indexes and every tracked ETF are now negative, with rising long rates hitting small caps and real estate hardest. WTI's $93.29 quote differs from the midnight, morning, and noon snapshots of $93.84, $95.74, and $95.39, requiring timestamp, baseline, feed, or futures-contract reconciliation. PAC's death is repeatedly attributed across wrestling sources, but the dispatch itself notes pending autopsy results, so biographical certainty should remain qualified.
