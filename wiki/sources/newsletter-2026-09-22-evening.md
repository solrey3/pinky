## Synthesis Notes

This dispatch connects AI governance to two concrete deployment environments. Aviation automation requires bounded authority, human override, monitoring, and safe fallback; cybercrime disruption requires identity controls, credential protection, infrastructure intervention, and investigation of the full tool-and-account ecosystem. The Microsoft item also broadens agentic security beyond autonomous model failures: malicious operators can deliberately use AI systems to increase the scale and accessibility of conventional account compromise.

The market snapshot remains concentrated risk-on rather than broadly confirmed strength. Semiconductors and crypto surge while the Dow, defensive exposures, real estate, and crude weaken.
