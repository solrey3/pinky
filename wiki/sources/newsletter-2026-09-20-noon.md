## Synthesis Notes

The dispatch extends two different control-and-access threads. PlayStation's physical-media retreat centralizes consumer access in a platform, while supply-chain attackers exploit centralized trust in repositories, maintainers, and build systems. In both cases, durable rights and trustworthy intermediaries become more important as control moves away from end users.

The $96.08 WTI quote and most equity figures repeat the morning dispatch, while Bitcoin's reported gain changes from 5.66% to 6.21%. These should be treated as overlapping fetched snapshots rather than independent sessions. Iran's renewed negotiating conditions and Saudi pipeline coverage also show why a falling crude quote does not eliminate geopolitical tail risk or immediately reverse diesel-price pressure.
