## Summary

The August 26 close remains dispersed near annual highs: the Dow is flat while the S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Russell 2000 decline, with technology and small caps weakest. [[WTI]] falls 5.92% to $81.91, Nvidia, Amazon, Tesla, and Ralph Lauren retreat, while international equities, AMD, UPS, Berkshire Hathaway, and Bitcoin advance. This extends [[Risk-Off Market Rotation]] as selective repricing rather than uniform liquidation.

The news ledger centers on [[Meta]] reaching a reported $18 billion settlement with 48 states over child-safety claims, [[Canada]] imposing 50% tariffs on U.S. goods, an immigrant-visa processing pause, and a renewed warning that AI can either broaden opportunity or amplify injustice. CrowdStrike reports AI-fueled growth, Anthropic leads prediction-market odds for the largest 2026 IPO, and Tailcat demonstrates a netcat-like utility over Tailscale's data plane. Sports coverage records Matthew Brennan's second Vuelta stage win, [[Tadej Pogacar]] as the completed Tour de France champion, MLB finals, Devils updates, and wrestling schedules and results. Kalshi, local Wawa pricing, most team reports, and several league newswires remain explicitly unavailable.
