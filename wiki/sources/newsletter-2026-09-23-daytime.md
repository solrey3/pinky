The AI items connect commercial model competition with epistemic behavior: lower prices and newer releases do not by themselves establish reliability, while uncertainty expression is useful only when calibration is measured against outcomes.

The market tape again shows concentrated risk-on leadership rather than broad strength. Technology, semiconductors, and crypto surge while the Dow and several diversified or defensive exposures weaken. The WTI quote conflicts with the same day's midnight snapshot and should not be interpreted causally without contract and timestamp reconciliation.
