## Summary

The September 25 noon dispatch leads with mortgage rates reaching 7%, Oracle warning that it might not meet a data-center lease obligation, judicial restoration of White House access for reporters, continued immigration-enforcement scrutiny, and an appeals court upholding the Pentagon's Anthropic blacklist. High long-term borrowing costs and weak breadth therefore qualified the technology-led rally.
