## Summary

The September 21 evening brief follows several contests over institutional authority. OpenAI called for U.S.-led global AI safety standards, while consumer AI agents continued climbing app charts and seeking purchasing authority. ## Synthesis Notes

The dispatch connects corporate consolidation, AI rulemaking, delegated purchasing, press access, and immigration enforcement through a recurring governance problem: power needs a defined mandate, reviewable boundaries, and an effective challenge mechanism. Settlement can unblock concentration without resolving every public-interest concern; international AI standards need institutional legitimacy and enforceability; autonomous purchasing needs scoped authorization and liability; and executive access or enforcement rules remain constrained by courts and constitutional protections.

The closing tape is concentrated risk-on leadership, not broad confirmation. Technology and crypto surged while blue chips, small caps, dividends, value, real estate, and crude weakened. The progression from noon to evening should be retained as timestamped snapshots rather than combined into a single clean daily move.
