## Summary

The September 12 midnight dispatch forecasts clouds and later showers for Toms River and continued overcast weather in Makati. The security lead says [[Anthropic]] reported that [[Iran]] used an American AI model to target U.S. Navy warships, placing frontier-model misuse directly in the national-security and dual-use governance frame. Markets ended the week broadly risk-off: every tracked major index and ETF declined, the Dow and Russell 2000 lost more than 2%, and the 30-year Treasury yield rose to 5.354%, near its annual high. [[WTI]] jumped 7.48% to $99.99 while Bitcoin declined 1.50%. [[AMD]] surged 13.15% and TSM gained 3.89% against the wider selloff. Sports coverage records Eddie Dunbar's Vuelta Stage 19 victory, Enric Mas's decisive Stage 20 test, a full MLB scoreboard, and AAA TripleMania 34 and WWE title developments.
