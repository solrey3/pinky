## Synthesis Notes

Apple's permission change continues the [[Agentic Access Control]] thread at the operating-system boundary: local AI agents should receive explicit, narrow, observable, and revocable file permissions rather than inherit a user's ambient disk authority. The Flock ruling applies the same concern to institutional searches: scalable databases magnify Fourth Amendment risk when query authority, purpose, retention, and judicial oversight are weak.

The reported workplace layoffs move [[AI Labor Displacement]] from forecasts toward claimed realized effects, but the dispatch provides no employer counts, occupations, causal method, or displacement baseline. The Smithsonian item supplies a constructive counterpoint in which AI supports metadata discovery rather than replacing expert historical judgment.

The market board repeats the day's completed-week figures rather than establishing a fresh session. Semiconductor gains keep the Nasdaq positive, while broad fund losses, Dow weakness, and a near-peak long yield show weak breadth.
