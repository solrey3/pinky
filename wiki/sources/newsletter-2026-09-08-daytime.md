## Summary

The September 8 morning dispatch contrasts sunny Toms River with showers in Makati and leads on an encouraging interpretation of the August jobs report. Other headlines include a reported surge of more than 30,000% in pediatric GLP-1 prescriptions since 2019, a threatened U.S. ban on Bombardier aircraft as the [[Canada]] tariff deadline approaches, elevated gasoline prices amid the [[Iran]] war, Second Amendment briefing, and renewed attention to Chinese financial statecraft.

Technology coverage reports a complicated ownership structure behind a $3.2 billion AI data center, a German company's claimed first fully commercial European orbital launch, and the factoring of legacy RSA keys once used by a 1990s certificate authority. Markets are nearly flat at index level but highly dispersed: the Nasdaq rises 0.40% while the Dow falls 0.27%; [[Nvidia]] gains 5.89%, international equities approach their annual high, real estate and several consumer names weaken, Bitcoin falls 1.21%, and WTI rises 2.98%. The 30-year Treasury yield remains elevated at 5.246%, mortgage rates are 6.91%, and gasoline exceeds $4 nationally and in New Jersey.

Sports coverage records [[Mathieu van der Poel]] as Tour de France Stage 21 winner and [[Tadej Pogacar]] as general-classification champion by 6:26 over Remco Evenepoel; Pogacar's absence is also framed as improving the U.S. men's road-race opportunity. The Mets beat Miami 9–4, Raw coverage notes Liv Morgan retaining and Bayley returning, and the wrestling ledger repeats highly rated All In and Stardom matches. Several sports feeds, team reports, local fuel data, and Kalshi data are explicitly unavailable.
