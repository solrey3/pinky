## Summary

The August 21 morning dispatch opens with heavy rain and a cool 66°F daybreak in [[Toms River]], contrasted with hot, windy, patchy rain in [[Makati]]. All four major U.S. indexes fell about 2% or more, led by the Nasdaq at -2.75%, while the 30-year Treasury yield remained elevated at 5.237%. The move was highly uneven: Bitcoin jumped 20.67%, oil and SCHD rose, and Netflix, Tesla, and PayPal advanced despite broad equity and semiconductor weakness.

The news ledger emphasizes pressure on households and public institutions. Colleges face an enrollment squeeze from demographic contraction; workers face rising employer-healthcare costs; Walmart reported its slowest sales growth in six years; and possible FCC E-Rate cuts threaten school and library connectivity.
