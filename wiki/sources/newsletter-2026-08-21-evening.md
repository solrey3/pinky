## Summary

The August 21 evening brief pairs overcast conditions in [[Toms River]] with continuing showers in [[Makati]]. U.S. equities closed broadly lower: the S&P 500 fell 1.43%, the Dow 0.85%, the Nasdaq 2.05%, and the Russell 2000 1.65%. The selloff remained internally divided. [[Bitcoin]] surged 21.64%, WTI rose 2.53%, SCHD gained 1.71%, and [[Tesla]] jumped 6.02% after regulatory clearance advanced a Las Vegas robotaxi launch, while AMD fell 8.00% and Nvidia 4.64%. Long financing costs remained restrictive, with the 30-year Treasury at 5.276% and the average 30-year mortgage at 6.72%.

The news ledger centers on the reliability of institutional signals. Lawmakers are considering restrictions on ghost-job advertisements that may represent no genuine vacancy; Walmart reported its slowest sales growth in six years; and appeals rulings sharply changed damages in the Alex Jones and Project Veritas disputes. Researchers also demonstrated that thunder vibrations captured through fiber-optic cable can reveal near-surface Earth structure, while Cobalt extended Kobo e-readers into app-capable devices.

Sports coverage records nine MLB finals and one game in progress, the completed Tour de France results for [[Mathieu van der Poel]] and [[Tadej Pogacar]], Vuelta and Deutschland Tour updates, a Devils coaching appointment, and wrestling schedules and headlines. Verified prediction-market movements, Kalshi data, local Wawa fuel pricing, several sports newswires, and Devils schedule data remained explicitly unavailable.

## Synthesis Notes

This close extends the day's split-market pattern: technology-heavy indexes and semiconductors weaken while crypto, oil, dividends, and a regulation-sensitive autonomy stock rally. The ghost-job and missing-feed stories share a deeper concern about signal integrity: a published listing or empty field should not be treated as evidence of a real opportunity or observed value without provenance. Fiber-optic seismic imaging provides the constructive counterpart—existing infrastructure becomes more valuable when its signals are measurable, interpretable, and tied to a verifiable physical event.
