## Summary

The September 11 morning dispatch forecasts a warm, damp day in Toms River and rain in Makati. On the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks, its lead news combines memorial coverage with an [[Anthropic]] researcher's reported resignation over fears that advanced AI could escape effective control, continued [[Iran]] ballistic-missile production, changing U.S. public opinion since 2001, and political attention shifting toward the 2028 presidential race.

Technology coverage says Europe will continue a Venus mission after [[NASA]] withdrew a radar instrument and reports that [[Shopify]] is moving mobile development from React Native back to Swift and Kotlin. The latter is a large-platform case for [[Native Mobile Development]]: shared-code efficiency may eventually be outweighed by platform fidelity, maintainability, performance, and access to operating-system capabilities.

Markets were broadly risk-off. All four tracked U.S. indexes and every tracked ETF declined, led by the Russell 2000 at 2.11% and the Dow at 1.88%. Bitcoin fell 2.66%, while [[WTI]] surged 7.16% to $99.69 and diesel reportedly reached $6 per gallon, sharpening inflation risk. AMD's 10.18% gain and TSM's 3.02% rise were notable exceptions. Sports coverage records Stefan Küng's Vuelta Stage 18 time-trial win, Enric Mas retaining the race lead, and the previously reported Tour de France winners.
