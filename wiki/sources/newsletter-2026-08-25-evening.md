## Summary

The August 25 close is mixed near index highs: the Dow gains 0.44%, while the S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Russell 2000 decline modestly. Beneath that calm surface, [[WTI]] falls 7.65% to $81.11 and major chip stocks weaken, while dividends, international equities, real estate, Netflix, Tesla, PayPal, and UPS advance. This extends [[Risk-Off Market Rotation]] as a dispersed repricing rather than a uniform flight from risk.

The news ledger combines growing resistance to [[Flock Safety]] license-plate cameras, Canadian retaliation against U.S. tariffs, sanctions shifting U.S.–Iran competition toward Iraq, a child-safety lawsuit against Snapchat, and proposed wartime rent controls in California. [[Apple]] introduces M6 and M5 Ultra around performance and AI compute, while the FDA authorizes continuous wearable monitoring of ketones and glucose. Sports coverage records [[Tadej Pogacar]] winning Vuelta Stage 4 through a 50 km solo attack, MLB results, Devils updates, and wrestling schedules and headlines. Prediction-market coverage prices Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez at 21% for the 2028 Democratic nomination but provides no movement figure, while Kalshi remains unavailable.
