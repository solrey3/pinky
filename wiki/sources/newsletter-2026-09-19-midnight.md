## Synthesis Notes

The military-intelligence item extends the autonomy thread upstream from weapons operation to the information pipeline that informs attack decisions. Human authorization is not a sufficient safeguard if decision-makers receive fabricated or weakly validated machine-generated intelligence; provenance, independent corroboration, uncertainty communication, and abort procedures are part of meaningful human control.

The FAA proposal shows the same assurance problem outside combat. As AI enters air-traffic congestion management, procurement must evaluate failure modes, monitoring, fallback operations, auditability, and operator authority rather than treating aggregate benchmark accuracy as enough.

The market board repeats the prior evening's narrow-growth pattern while sharpening crypto–energy divergence: semiconductor and Nasdaq strength coexist with weak small caps, value, dividends, international equities, and real estate. The repeated $95.47 WTI quote is consistent with the prior evening and should be treated as the same market snapshot unless timestamps establish otherwise.
