## Summary

The August 22 evening brief records clearing drizzle in [[Toms River]] and continuing showers in [[Makati]], then closes a split market week. All four major U.S. indexes fell, led by the Nasdaq at 2.05%, while AMD dropped 8.00% and Nvidia lost 4.64%. Against that equity weakness, [[Bitcoin]] surged 18.89%, WTI rose 3.03%, Tesla gained 6.02%, and SCHD advanced 1.71%. Long-term financing remained expensive, with the 30-year Treasury at 5.276% and the average 30-year mortgage at 6.72%.

The news ledger emphasizes trust, infrastructure, and policy consequences. State lawmakers considered bans on ghost-job advertisements; failed U.S.–[[Canada]] trade talks produced another tariff round and immediate Canadian retaliation; Walmart reported its slowest sales growth in six years; and local opposition to data centers entered midterm campaigns. Technology coverage paired research suggesting memories can survive major hibernation-related synapse loss with a federal push for more spaceports and the announced deprecation of `hdiutil` in macOS 27.

Sports coverage records [[Tadej Pogacar]] winning the Vuelta opening time trial and first leader's jersey, [[Mathieu van der Poel]] as the completed Tour de France Stage 21 winner, a full MLB scoreboard, and ROH Death Before Dishonor results. Prediction-market moves, Kalshi data, local Wawa pricing, many league feeds, schedules, and broadcast details remained explicitly unavailable.
