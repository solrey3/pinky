## Synthesis Notes

The AI stories connect technical intervention controls to public governance. A useful emergency stop requires authority over compute, model weights, deployment infrastructure, and operators; a statutory moratorium additionally requires measurable capability thresholds, international coordination, audit access, and due process. Neither headline establishes that a universal kill switch is technically feasible or that an ASI ban has operative legislative text.

The impeachment update narrows the prosecution from four charges to one, making the surviving allegation and the court's procedural reasoning more important than the raw dismissal count. The El Niño message continues earlier drought-preparation coverage, but household water storage is only one layer of resilience and does not substitute for utility planning, allocation rules, and public-health guidance.

The index and equity board exactly repeats the midnight snapshot, so no new equity move should be inferred.
