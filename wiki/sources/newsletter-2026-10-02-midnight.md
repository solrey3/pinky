## Synthesis Notes

The rates board repeats the recent divergence between softer inflation-based policy expectations and restrictive market borrowing costs. Reduced expectations for another policy hike do not mechanically lower long Treasury yields or mortgage quotes, especially when term-premium and credit-pricing forces remain elevated.

The equity snapshot extends broad weakness with narrow semiconductor exceptions rather than a clean defensive rotation. Because the index, ETF, yield, and many stock values repeat the prior evening snapshot, the dispatch may be carrying forward a market close rather than presenting a wholly new session.

WTI's $92.69 quote is lower than the prior evening's $92.91 while its displayed change is positive. That internal comparison should remain unresolved until timestamp, baseline, feed, and contract metadata are available.
