## Synthesis Notes

The pipeline, HBM expansion, and FOSS-funding argument share a resilience theme: systems become fragile when they depend on one chokepoint, one constrained component class, or maintainers whose labor is treated as free. Redundant energy routes, additional memory capacity, and sustainable maintenance funding all buy resilience, although each shifts costs and control rather than eliminating them.
