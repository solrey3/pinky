## Summary

The August 21 midnight dispatch pairs heavy rain, wind, and an 85% Friday rain chance in [[Toms River]] with warm, windy, patchy rain in [[Makati]]. All four major U.S. indexes fell about 2% or more, led by the Nasdaq at -2.75%, while the 30-year Treasury yield remained elevated at 5.237%. The selloff was not uniform: Bitcoin jumped 15.62%, oil rose 2.47%, SCHD gained, and Netflix, Tesla, and PayPal advanced despite broad equity and semiconductor weakness.

News coverage centers on household and institutional pressure. Colleges face a demographic enrollment squeeze, workers face rising employer-healthcare costs, and possible FCC E-Rate cuts threaten school and library connectivity. [[Moderna]] and [[Merck]] reported initial late-stage melanoma data for a personalized cancer vaccine, while an East Palestine report, federal debt planning, censorship demands, and tornado warnings emphasize transparency, fiscal, civil-liberties, and safety concerns. [[GitHub]] published its account of the August 17 outage, and a rare-book preservation plea argued for scanning vulnerable physical collections before they disappear.

Sports coverage records [[Tadej Pogacar]] targeting a Grand Tour sweep while retaining the Tour de France title, [[Mathieu van der Poel]] retaining the Stage 21 result, cycling stage wins, nine MLB finals, a Devils coaching appointment, and wrestling schedules and personnel news. Reliable prediction prices, Kalshi data, local Wawa pricing, most league feeds, team schedules, and broadcast details remained explicitly unavailable.
