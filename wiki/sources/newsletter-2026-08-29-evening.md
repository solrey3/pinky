## Summary

The August 29 evening brief reports a sunny 83°F day giving way to a cool night in [[Toms River]], while [[Makati]] remains showery and windy. Lead stories span monetary policy, civil liberties, surveillance, and technology: Kevin Warsh says the [[Federal Reserve]] may still need tighter policy; a federal court reportedly rejects speech-based deportation decisions; cities are terminating [[Flock Safety]] camera contracts at a record pace; and a Ninth Circuit ruling treats [[Kalshi]] sports contracts as gambling rather than swaps. Tencent's Hy4 Preview and vLLM 0.28.0 add open-source AI-model and inference-engine updates.

Large-cap indexes close higher and near annual highs, led by Nasdaq at +0.85%, while the Russell 2000 falls 1.51%. Most tracked ETFs decline, WTI falls 1.89%, Tesla loses 3.89%, and [[PayPal]] drops 12.82%, even as Amazon, Netflix, UPS, Nvidia, and Berkshire Hathaway rise. In cycling, [[Tadej Pogacar]] abandons the Vuelta after a Stage 8 crash reportedly caused a broken collarbone and concussion; Bryan Coquard wins the crash-marred stage. The completed Tour ledger still records Pogacar as general-classification champion. MLB scores, wrestling schedules and ratings, team links, and social feeds round out the dispatch.
