## Synthesis Notes

The Philippine thread continues to connect impeachment politics with public-works accountability, but transaction flags, flood-control reports, legislative allegations, and adjudicated findings are different evidentiary stages. The NCAP item adds a separate governance question: automated enforcement should be assessed for legal authority, calibration, notice, appeals, data retention, and operational readiness before reimplementation.

The AI-glasses, GrapheneOS, and Cloudflare items locate governance at three layers. Governments can constrain wearable collection and use; security-focused operating systems can refuse hardware that fails their standards; and infrastructure vendors can expose search as an API that downstream agents may consume. Each layer needs separate controls for sensing, device trust, provenance, access, logging, and downstream use.

The market move is concentrated risk-on rather than broad confirmation: semiconductors and growth surge while the Dow and several diversified exposures decline, and the long bond sits at the top of its displayed annual range. WTI's $90.59 quote is $0.08 below the same day's morning snapshot and $0.51 above midnight; missing timestamps and contract metadata prevent a clean intraday sequence.
