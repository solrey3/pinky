## Synthesis Notes

The procedural defeat reinforces that digital-asset policy remains exposed to coalition and sequencing risk even after markets have priced expected regulatory clarity. House action on Iran war powers places the same legislative-capacity question in a different domain: Congress can signal institutional authority, but passage in one chamber does not by itself settle policy or implementation.

The Medicare report strengthens the case that healthcare automation must be evaluated through denial rates, appealability, and patient access rather than efficiency claims alone. In markets, simultaneous small-cap, technology, international-equity, value, dividend, and real-estate losses indicate broad de-risking, while higher oil and near-peak yields tighten conditions further. A few positive stocks preserve dispersion but do not change the overall regime.
