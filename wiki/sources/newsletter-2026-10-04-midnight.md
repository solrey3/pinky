## Synthesis Notes

Apple's permission change and the call for hard budget caps describe complementary forms of bounded agency. [[Agentic Access Control]] limits what an autonomous system may reach; resource ceilings limit how much it may spend or consume before a human must authorize continuation. Both controls should be explicit, observable, and independently enforceable rather than left to model judgment.

The Flock ruling continues the shift from policy criticism and misuse allegations toward constitutional scrutiny. At network scale, [[Automated License-Plate Reader Surveillance]] makes query authority, purpose limitation, retention, immutable logs, judicial oversight, and remedies central civil-liberties controls. The Smithsonian item supplies a constructive AI use case in which machine-assisted discovery augments rather than displaces expert interpretation.

The market board is a repeated completed-week snapshot, not a fresh trading session.
