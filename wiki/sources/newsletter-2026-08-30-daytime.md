## Summary

The August 30 morning dispatch forecasts a warm, mostly dry Sunday for [[Toms River]] and continued rain and strong wind in [[Makati]]. Its lead stories emphasize institutional boundaries: Kevin Warsh says the [[Federal Reserve]] may still need to fight inflation; law-enforcement leaders debate the public-safety value and privacy cost of [[Flock Safety]] cameras; and a federal court reportedly holds that protected speech cannot justify deporting noncitizens. A separate ruling treats [[Kalshi]] sports contracts as gambling rather than swaps, while CPython adds official RISC-V support.

The completed-week market board is positive for large-cap indexes but weak underneath. Nasdaq gains 0.85%, the S&P 500 0.49%, and the Dow 0.53%, while the Russell 2000 falls 1.51%, every tracked ETF declines, and [[PayPal]] drops 12.82%. EFFR remains 3.63% within a 3.50%–3.75% target range, the 30-year Treasury stands at 5.206%, and PHP/USD is near the weak end of the peso's annual range. The newsletter therefore identifies market breadth, expensive financing, and currency pressure as the coming week's main watchpoints.

In cycling, [[Tadej Pogacar]] abandons the Vuelta after a Stage 8 crash reported as causing a neck fracture and concussion, with Bryan Coquard winning the stage. MLB coverage records a Mets 6–2 victory over Houston, while wrestling previews AEW All In, NXT Heatwave, and AAA Ola de Calor. Prediction-market prices, local Wawa fuel data, schedules, and several sports feeds are explicitly marked unavailable rather than interpreted as zero activity.
