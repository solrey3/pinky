## Summary

The August 31 midnight dispatch forecasts improving weather in [[Toms River]] and continued storm risk in [[Makati]]. Its lead stories place El Niño on the global economic-risk watchlist, report a renewed U.S. strike on Iranian rocket launchers near the Strait of Hormuz, describe pressure on Hong Kong independent bookstores, and note a Grand Canyon flash-flood search. [[Netflix]] is also reported to be pursuing five NFL games, extending streaming competition into premium live sports.

The market board remains strong at the large-cap headline level but weak underneath. The S&P 500, Dow, and Nasdaq gain and sit near annual highs while the Russell 2000 falls 1.51%, all tracked diversified ETFs decline, Bitcoin loses 3.15%, and [[PayPal]] drops 12.82%. EFFR remains 3.63% inside a 3.50%–3.75% target range, yet renewed rate-hike concern and a 5.206% 30-year Treasury yield keep financing risk elevated. WTI rises 3.47% amid the Hormuz security story.

In sports, Enric Mas wins Vuelta Stage 9 while Pavel Sivakov narrowly misses a victory he sought for injured teammate [[Tadej Pogacar]]. The completed Tour ledger still records Pogacar as general-classification champion. MLB includes a 16–1 Yankees win over Boston, the Devils publish national-broadcast updates, and AEW All In coverage leads wrestling. Prediction-market odds, local Wawa pricing, several sports wires, and schedule details remain explicitly unavailable rather than inferred.
