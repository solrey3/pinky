## Summary

The August 28 midnight board carries forward a broad equity rally: the Dow gains 1.54%, Nasdaq 1.82%, S&P 500 1.18%, and Russell 2000 0.73%, leaving all four near their annual highs. [[Nvidia]] rises 5.13% and most tracked ETFs advance, but real estate, Amazon, Netflix, PayPal, Schwab, and Ralph Lauren fall. ## Synthesis Notes

The dispatch extends the August 27 rally without making it uniform: high index levels coexist with expensive long-term financing, weaker real estate, declining oil, and mixed single-stock performance. The Anthropic ruling and Meta settlement put two different governance mechanisms beside each other—judicial limits on state procurement power and financial liability for platform harm. Physical-world agent interfaces make least privilege, fail-safe states, human override, and audit logs safety requirements rather than merely enterprise-security preferences. Smaller models may reduce cost and deployment dependence, but capability claims still need task-specific evidence. The repeated settlement discrepancy and extensive feed gaps reinforce that provenance and explicit uncertainty are part of the record.
