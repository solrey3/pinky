## Summary

The September 13 evening brief leads with reported intervention by Defense Secretary [[Pete Hegseth]] in eight senior Navy promotions, sticky August inflation and expectations of tighter Federal Reserve policy, threats to the Strait of Hormuz and Red Sea, a Georgia ballot-privacy dispute, a promised $5,000 federal dividend, and [[Connected-Car Surveillance]] concerns. The technology desk adds a consumer robot-dog review, a report that an AI model solved the 370-year-old Cyphral Distich, and criticism of Google's ad quality. These are newsletter-level leads whose political, military, technical, and legal claims require confirmation from linked reporting or primary records.

Markets ended broadly lower: all four tracked indexes and all five ETFs declined, led by the Russell 2000 at 2.17% and the Dow at 2.07%. [[WTI]] surged 10.49% to $102.79, the 30-year Treasury yield reached 5.354%, and the quoted mortgage rate stood at 6.91%, combining energy and borrowing-cost pressure. [[AMD]] gained 13.15% and TSM rose 3.89% despite the broad weakness, illustrating a narrow semiconductor exception within a [[Risk-Off Market Rotation]].

Sports coverage reports Enric Mas winning the Vuelta a España, Alexander Zverev winning the US Open, Europe taking the Solheim Cup, and Tadej Pogacar remaining the recorded Tour de France champion. The wrestling desk records Dominik Mysterio retaining at AAA TripleMania 34 Night 2 and reports that [[Stephanie Vaquer]] won WWE's Women's World Championship in Chile.
