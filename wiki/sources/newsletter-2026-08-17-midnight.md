## Synthesis Notes

This edition reinforces a common evidence-governance pattern. Conversational fluency does not establish clinical safety, popularity does not erase foodborne risk, and strong model answers do not make excess reasoning free. Judicial review supplies the institutional analogue: consequential authority needs independent checks. Market timestamps and explicit missing-data markers apply the same principle operationally by keeping confidence bounded by verifiable evidence.
