## Summary

The September 19 noon dispatch leads with Warren Buffett's reported departure as Berkshire Hathaway chairman, alongside White House press-access restrictions, cautious Danish reaction to a U.S.–Greenland security agreement, a proposed $2.6 billion U.S. air-defense sale to Ukraine, and scrutiny of CBP facial-recognition targeting.
