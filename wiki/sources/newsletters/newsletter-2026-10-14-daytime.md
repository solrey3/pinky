## Synthesis Notes

The Flock item extends the wiki's surveillance thread from procurement backlash and warrantless-query rulings to judicial warnings about network-scale constitutional harm. The recurring governance requirements remain warrants or clear legal authorization, purpose-limited queries, short retention, immutable logs, independent review, accuracy controls, and effective remedies.

Apple's reported Homeview hub fits its broader platform-control strategy: a household screen can become the interface through which devices, services, identity, automation, and data flows are coordinated. The newsletter does not establish specifications or availability, so the strategic interpretation should remain provisional pending Apple documentation.
