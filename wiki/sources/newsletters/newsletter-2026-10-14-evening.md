## Summary

The October 14 evening brief leads with higher September inflation attributed to gasoline, a reported Character.AI Chapter 11 process that could benefit Disney, litigation by states alleging that OpenAI misrepresented safety, an appellate ruling favoring Florida restrictions on addictive social-media features for minors, and election-law and Jerusalem developments. ## Synthesis Notes

The OpenAI lawsuit report extends model-safety governance beyond published policies and benchmark claims. Legal review can test whether safety representations match internal evaluations, known incidents, release decisions, monitoring, and remedies, but the complaint, parties, causes of action, and procedural status need docket confirmation before conclusions are drawn.

The NASA and Character.AI items share a continuity theme: programs and assets can survive institutional distress by finding renewed funding, political support, or a buyer. Survival does not itself establish mission readiness, transaction completion, or strategic value; those require primary records and execution evidence.
