## Summary

The August 27 noon tape is broadly bullish near annual highs: the Dow gains 1.63%, Nasdaq 1.54%, S&P 500 1.09%, and Russell 2000 0.72%. [[Nvidia]] rises 4.42% as its earnings put the durability of the AI rally under scrutiny, while Salesforce reportedly jumps 20%. The advance is not uniform—Amazon, Netflix, PayPal, Schwab, Ralph Lauren, and real estate fall, and [[WTI]] declines 2.89%. The 30-year Treasury remains elevated at 5.179%, mortgages are quoted at 6.73%, and coverage of an unclear inflation stance before Jackson Hole adds a [[Monetary Policy Forward Guidance]] problem to an otherwise risk-on market.

The news ledger repeats [[Meta]]'s reported $18 billion settlement over teen-safety claims, describes improving but obstacle-filled U.S.–China summit diplomacy, and presents evidence that Central American cacao reached Georgia roughly 1,000 years ago. Sports coverage records [[Tadej Pogacar]] surviving a near-crash to win Vuelta Stage 6—his third victory of the race—while preserving his completed Tour de France title. MLB finals, Devils broadcast updates, wrestling schedules and injury reports, and local Asbury Park restoration funding round out the dispatch. Newsweek, prediction-market movement, local Wawa fuel pricing, and several sports feeds remain explicitly unavailable.
