## Summary

The September 20 morning dispatch leads with Warren Buffett's reported departure as Berkshire Hathaway chairman and President Trump's proposed “AI Force” and “AI Czar,” placing corporate succession and centralized federal AI coordination side by side. ## Synthesis Notes

The dispatch connects centralized control across government and media. A proposed White House AI command structure could improve coordination but also concentrates agenda-setting and accountability, while PlayStation's move away from discs shifts preservation, resale, access, and continuity toward platform operators. Both developments make governance rules and durable user rights more important as control moves upward.

The market board repeats the midnight dispatch's $96.08 WTI quote and approximately 5.7% Bitcoin gain, suggesting the same or a closely adjacent snapshot. The sharp oil decline does not immediately erase diesel and freight pressure because retail fuel prices lag commodity moves and reflect refining, distribution, taxes, and regional constraints. As with the prior dispatch, quote provenance and timestamps should be retained before interpreting the percentage moves as a new session.
