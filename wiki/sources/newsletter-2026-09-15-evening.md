## Summary

The September 15 evening brief combines policy setbacks with a pronounced risk-off close. A landmark digital-asset bill reportedly failed a procedural vote, clouding the path for new U.S. crypto market rules, while reports of record household income and continuing public support for political compromise offered more constructive domestic signals. The technology desk says [[SpaceX]] declared Starship ready for Flight 14 and highlights workplace-safety behavior in a humanoid robot, an automated bird-illustration project, and an Internet Archive access update.

All four tracked equity indexes and all five tracked ETFs declined. ## Synthesis Notes

The failed procedural vote shows that crypto-policy expectations are sensitive not only to bill text but also to coalition discipline and legislative sequencing. This reverses the optimistic policy catalyst recorded in August: anticipated regulatory clarity can support repricing before enactment, but procedural failure can quickly restore uncertainty.

The close extends the recent yield-pressured risk-off pattern. Small caps again underperformed, semiconductors sold off sharply, and diversified ETFs offered little shelter, while rising oil and near-peak Treasury rates tightened the macro backdrop. Selective gains in quality, financial, logistics, and consumer names show dispersion remained, but the weakness was broader than a single AI-stock correction.
