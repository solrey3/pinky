## Synthesis Notes

Amazon's proposed buildout extends the wiki's infrastructure theme beyond electricity demand and corporate capex. Local opposition makes social license, land use, water, grid capacity, and benefit sharing practical constraints on data-center scale rather than peripheral political concerns.

The Utah VPN ruling sharpens the age-verification problem: a policy can pursue child safety while assigning intermediaries an identity-detection task that encrypted network architecture cannot reliably perform. Effective regulation therefore depends on technical feasibility as well as privacy, proportionality, and enforcement design.
