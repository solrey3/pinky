## Summary

The September 13 midnight dispatch forecasts Sunday rain in Toms River and a warm, partly rainy day in Makati. Its lead coverage says steady August inflation moved expectations toward a Federal Reserve rate increase. Other headlines report a Houthi seizure of a strategically important Red Sea island, an industry shift toward AI-safety concerns, an El Niño-linked record-slow Atlantic hurricane season, Ukraine's military innovation, and Arch Manning's apparent wrist injury.

Technology and business coverage highlights a $4,000 Chinese robot dog, an easier OpenStreetMap editing workflow, and Real-SWE, a benchmark intended to evaluate AI software engineering on private enterprise code. [[OpenAI]] reportedly ruled out a 2026 IPO, while Elon Musk backed [[Anthropic]]'s call to slow AI development. These are brief secondary-source summaries rather than independently verified corporate or technical findings.

The completed week remained broadly risk-off: all four tracked indexes and every tracked ETF declined, led by the Russell 2000 at 2.17% and Dow at 2.07%. The 30-year Treasury yield reached 5.354%, mortgages were quoted at 6.91%, and [[WTI]] rose 7.55% to $100.05. [[AMD]] gained 13.15% and TSM rose 3.89% despite broad weakness, while Nvidia fell 4.45% and Bitcoin declined 1.38%. Sports coverage records Mikel Landa winning Vuelta Stage 20 while Enric Mas retained the lead, Elena Rybakina becoming tennis world No. 1, a large MLB scoreboard, and current wrestling results and schedules.
