## Synthesis Notes

The dispatch extends the prior evening's central pattern rather than overturning it: headline indexes and the weekly tape are positive, but small caps, diversified funds, oil, Bitcoin, Tesla, and especially PayPal show that gains are not broad. Warsh's warning preserves a tightening possibility even with a stable current policy rate and already-expensive long borrowing. The East Palestine and Nepal stories make provenance operational rather than archival—risk models, climate evidence, and response records must be disclosed in time to support accountable decisions. ICE's proposed robotic dogs similarly show that new technical capability requires explicit authority, use limits, audit trails, and civil-liberties review. Across markets, public safety, enforcement technology, sports, and feed operations, confidence should remain proportional to transparent evidence and clearly marked uncertainty.
