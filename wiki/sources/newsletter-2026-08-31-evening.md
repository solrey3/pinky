## Summary

The August 31 evening brief reports an overcast 76°F in [[Toms River]] before a hot 92°F Tuesday, while [[Makati]] remains overcast with nearby-rain risk. National and international headlines include booming large-company profits, a reported [[Federal Trade Commission]] lawsuit alleging [[Amazon]] deceived advertisers, unresolved questions around a U.S.–Venezuela oil arrangement, divided civilian responses to Ukrainian attacks in occupied Crimea, delayed mifepristone litigation, and the detention of a 76-year-old green-card holder with Stage 4 cancer.

Technology coverage says Anthropic staff messages praising piracy were cited in Sony litigation, Google removed Manifest V2 extensions—including uBlock Origin—from the Chrome Web Store, and BirdNET Go can repurpose security cameras for automated bird identification. These are headline-level claims: the Anthropic link points to an older settlement article, so the exact litigation context needs primary-source verification.

Markets remain internally divided. The S&P 500 gains 0.43% and the Nasdaq 1.50%, supported by Nvidia, AMD, and Tesla, while the Dow loses 0.43%, the Russell 2000 falls 1.29%, and most tracked domestic ETFs decline. [[PayPal]] plunges 14.62%, the issue's major-move alert, while WTI rises 4.96% and Bitcoin falls 1.57%. In sports, [[Tadej Pogacar]] is reported to have undergone successful clavicle surgery and to remain in Barcelona; the completed Tour ledger still lists him as champion. MLB results, wrestling schedules and headlines, and recent Meltzer ratings are included, but prediction markets, local Wawa pricing, several sports wires, and team schedules remain unavailable.
