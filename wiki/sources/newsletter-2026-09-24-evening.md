## Summary

The September 24 evening brief leads with Donald Trump opening White House talks with Xi Jinping, 30-year mortgage rates above 7%, possible U.S. limits on diesel exports, continued uncertainty over White House television-pool access, and criticism of the gap between OpenAI's safety claims and a reported Australian incident. Technology coverage adds F-Droid 2.0 and New York's lawsuit seeking to stop Polymarket from operating as an allegedly illegal gambling platform.

Markets remained divided despite gains in headline technology indexes. The Nasdaq rose 1.97%, the S&P 500 gained 0.87%, AMD jumped 15.44%, and Bitcoin added 3.90%, while the Dow fell 0.83%, the Russell 2000 lost 1.36%, and every tracked diversified ETF declined. The 30-year Treasury yield reached 5.461%, the listed mortgage rate was 7.17%, and WTI fell 1.06% to $94.76. Near-peak long rates and weakness outside large-cap technology kept the close from reading as broad risk appetite.
