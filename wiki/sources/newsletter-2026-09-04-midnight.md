## Summary

The September 4 midnight dispatch repeats two major platform stories: [[Nvidia]] reportedly agrees to acquire [[Hugging Face]] for $13 billion, potentially linking dominant AI hardware with a major model-and-developer distribution layer, while [[Google]] avoids forced divestiture of its ad exchange despite an illegal-monopoly finding. A public-health item alleges that federal leadership ordered two Pennsylvania measles deaths removed from public reporting even though the CDC continued counting them internally, raising both [[Measles Resurgence]] and institutional-transparency concerns.

Markets remain calm at the large-cap index level but sharply divided underneath. The S&P 500, Dow, and Nasdaq each gain about 0.2%, while the Russell 2000 falls 1.53%. WTI surges 7.18% to $91.92, amplifying gasoline and inflation pressure; Bitcoin rises 2.91%, PayPal falls 7.56%, and Tesla gains 6.08%. The 30-year Treasury yield remains elevated at 5.243% and mortgage rates stand at 6.83%.

International and domestic coverage includes extended U.S. Middle East deployments, Israeli-Turkish tension in Syria, Maryland redistricting litigation, an ICE-arrest lawsuit, and pre–Labor Day gasoline increases. Sports coverage records [[Tadej Pogacar]] leaving hospital after his Vuelta crash with his World Championships status uncertain, Jakob Omrzel setting a Slovenian Grand Tour age record, MLB results, and wrestling schedules and headlines.
