## Synthesis Notes

This edition connects scarcity and scale. A shrinking river becomes harder to govern as claims accumulate; a surveillance network becomes riskier as access spreads; an automated fix becomes more consequential when it reaches privileged CI/CD paths; and Nvidia's capital concentration extends AI-market exposure into private space infrastructure. Across these domains, trustworthy governance requires bounded access, auditable decisions, explicit provenance, and evidence granular enough to reveal who bears the risk.
