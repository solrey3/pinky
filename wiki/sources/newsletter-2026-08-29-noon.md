## Summary

The August 29 noon dispatch reports sunny, mild conditions in [[Toms River]] and rain with strong winds in [[Makati]]. Its lead stories combine inflation, state power, climate risk, and surveillance: Kevin Warsh says the [[Federal Reserve]] may still need tighter policy; Donald Trump claims majority U.S. control over part of Venezuela's oil reserves; rescue and climate attribution work continues after catastrophic flooding in Nepal; and Texas bars state agencies from funding [[Flock Safety]] cameras.

Large-cap indexes remain near annual highs, led by Nasdaq at +0.85%, while the Russell 2000 falls 1.51%. The completed week is positive across all four indexes, but Friday's breadth is narrow: most tracked ETFs decline, WTI loses 1.89%, Tesla falls 3.89%, and [[PayPal]] drops 12.82%. EFFR remains 3.63% inside the 3.50%–3.75% target range, while the 30-year Treasury is 5.206% and the quoted mortgage rate is 6.74%.

Technology coverage adds a Ninth Circuit ruling that [[Kalshi]] sports contracts are gambling rather than swaps, Debian's qualified approval of responsible generative-AI use, and a renewed argument for using [[SQLite]] JSON support as a document database. In cycling, [[Tadej Pogacar]] abandons the Vuelta after a Stage 8 crash while Bryan Coquard wins the stage; the completed Tour ledger still records Pogacar as general-classification champion. MLB results, Devils links, wrestling schedules and ratings, and social feeds are preserved, with unavailable prediction-market and sports-feed data explicitly labeled.
