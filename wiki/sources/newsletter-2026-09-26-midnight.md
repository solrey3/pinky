## Summary

The September 26 midnight dispatch repeats two constitutional disputes: the Supreme Court reportedly revived federal use of an immigration database for voter checks, while a separate case challenges warrantless border searches of phones. International coverage emphasizes pressure on Black Sea grain exports and Iran's offensive posture. The technology desk adds an appellate ruling permitting the government to blacklist Anthropic, Oracle's warning about a data-center lease obligation, and worker resistance to training Tesla's Optimus robots as potential replacements.

Markets closed with concentrated technology strength rather than broad participation. The Nasdaq gained 2.06%, the S&P 500 rose 1.21%, and AMD jumped 12.65%, while the Russell 2000, dividend stocks, small-cap value, real estate, Bitcoin, and WTI declined. The 30-year Treasury yield and 90-day bill rate sat near their 52-week highs, and the listed 30-year mortgage rate reached 7.22%.

Sports coverage records Stephen Curry's reported two-year, $116 million Warriors extension, college football and baseball results, NHL preseason scores, soccer schedules, and local-team updates. Wrestling coverage centers on CMLL Noche de Campeones, Rey Pegasus's title win, and upcoming WWE, AEW, ROH, TNA, NJPW, and CMLL programming. Aggregated legal, political, market, injury, contract, and sports claims require primary-source confirmation.

This dispatch largely extends the September 25 evening cycle.
