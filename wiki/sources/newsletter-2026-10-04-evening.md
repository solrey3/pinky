## Synthesis Notes

The Supreme Court docket, ICC protocol change, lived-name proposal, hemp ruling, and teachers' protest all concern how institutions translate broad authority into procedures affecting rights and access. Their shared lesson is procedural: headline descriptions are insufficient without the underlying docket, order, protocol, or policy text.

The electrification and local-model items expose two kinds of infrastructure compression. Electrification moves bottlenecks from end-user adoption into generation, transmission, distribution, supply chains, and permitting; local AI attempts to compress model-serving demands into consumer hardware through quantization, offloading, or other implementation tradeoffs. Both require measurement of the full system rather than reliance on a capability headline.

Private-credit scrutiny and the market board also share a concentration theme. Hidden lending exposure can accumulate outside public markets while visible equity gains cluster in a few semiconductor names. The repeated $91.11 WTI quote and unchanged board indicate snapshot reuse, not an additional Sunday move.
