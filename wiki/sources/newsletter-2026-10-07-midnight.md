## Synthesis Notes

Default text watermarking in one regulatory region turns provenance from a general product feature into jurisdiction-specific compliance behavior. Its value depends on measurable detector performance, resistance to editing and translation, disclosure, appeal paths for false positives, and interoperability; a default setting alone is not proof of reliable attribution.

The reported streaming-service unification is an early post-closing integration signal. It may simplify access, but it also concentrates catalog control and creates open questions about pricing, account migration, content removal, bundling, and market power.
