## Synthesis Notes

This edition connects evidence quality to institutional legitimacy. Polished chatbot conversation is not proof of clinical benefit; a large surveillance network is not legitimate without enforceable limits; and executive action is not self-validating without review and due process. The same discipline applies to market and feed reporting: timestamps, granular components, missing-data markers, and auditable provenance prevent confident presentation from outrunning evidence.
