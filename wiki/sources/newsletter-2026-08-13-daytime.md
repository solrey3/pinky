## Synthesis Notes

This edition links macroeconomic, market, and technical signals through the problem of hidden internals. A cooler CPI headline does not erase elevated long rates; green headline indexes do not imply uniform breadth; and stable-looking software can conceal rare persistence or credential-path failures. The recurring lesson is to inspect transmission mechanisms—policy into borrowing costs, index moves into sector breadth, and software dependencies into build and database state—rather than relying on top-line status alone.
