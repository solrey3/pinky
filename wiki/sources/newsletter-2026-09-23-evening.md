## Synthesis Notes

The Flock allegation strengthens the wiki's standing conclusion that surveillance governance is fundamentally an authorization and audit problem. Even accurate plate data becomes dangerous when personnel can run personal queries without narrow purpose limits, supervisory review, immutable logs, and meaningful penalties.

Claude's reported enzyme-system discovery broadens Anthropic's record from frontier-model releases and formalized mathematics into biological hypothesis generation. The claim should remain provisional until methods, datasets, laboratory validation, reproducibility, and independent review are available; model novelty is not equivalent to scientific confirmation.

The market remained concentrated rather than broadly risk-on. Large-cap technology and semiconductors rallied while small caps, dividends, value, real estate, and financial names weakened and long rates stayed near annual highs. The evening AMD and WTI quotes differ from the same day's earlier dispatches and require timestamp, baseline, feed, or futures-contract reconciliation.
