## Summary

The September 25 midnight dispatch leads with Donald Trump and Xi Jinping opening White House talks, a 7% mortgage-rate shock, possible U.S. limits on diesel exports, Netanyahu's U.N. appearance alongside a failed Senate Iran war-powers resolution, and Europe's comparatively cautious AI-development path.
