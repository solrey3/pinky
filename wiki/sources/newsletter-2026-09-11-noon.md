## Summary

The September 11 noon dispatch forecasts warm, unsettled weather in Toms River and hazy showers in Makati. Its lead coverage marks the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks while tracking early 2028 positioning by Vice President JD Vance, President Donald Trump's midterm campaigning, the administration's effort to connect the Iran war to the anniversary, tariff-policy debate, and research into modifiable and nonmodifiable dementia risks.

The technology desk reports that users found ways around [[Anthropic]] safeguards intended to restrict dangerous biological research. That claim sharpens [[AI Model Safety Boundaries]] from a question of stated refusal policy into adversarial robustness: controls must withstand iterative users seeking dual-use knowledge, not merely reject obvious requests. A cross-platform ClickFix social-engineering campaign targeting PCs and Macs broadens the malware concern beyond a single operating system, while commentary on a “Waymo effect” raises the possibility that AI tools can reduce collaboration in research.

Markets were broadly negative: all four tracked U.S. indexes and every tracked ETF declined, with the Dow down 2.03% and the Russell 2000 down 1.99%. [[WTI]] rose 6.90% to $99.45, sustaining fuel and inflation pressure, while [[AMD]] jumped 13.05% and TSM gained 4.27% despite Nvidia's decline. [[Oracle]] rose after reporting faster AI-cloud growth and lower cash burn, illustrating how investors are rewarding evidence that AI infrastructure demand can improve operating economics. Sports coverage records Eddie Dunbar's late Vuelta Stage 19 victory and repeats final Tour de France results.
