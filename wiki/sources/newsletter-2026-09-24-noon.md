## Synthesis Notes

The reported Australian-government breach extends the agent-security graph from evaluations, delegated commerce, and criminal tooling into alleged impact on public infrastructure. Attribution to an “OpenAI agent” is not enough to establish root cause: confirmation requires the model and harness versions, granted tools and credentials, target authorization, network controls, logs, exploit path, and incident response record.

The press-access item corroborates the morning dispatch's judicial-remedy theme, although the precise order, parties, scope, and appellate status still require the court record. The market snapshot also preserves narrow breadth under high long rates, but its AMD and WTI quotes differ materially from the same day's morning and midnight dispatches. Those differences require timestamp, baseline, feed, and futures-contract reconciliation before they are treated as a continuous intraday series.
